Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 03, 2026

📄 This privacy policy is also published on the app stores. You can view the official version here: sites.google.com/view/number-buddies-privacy-policy/home
Number Buddies is designed with children's privacy as a top priority. We understand the importance of protecting children's personal information, especially for an app designed for users ages 3–6.

Information We Don't Collect

Number Buddies does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information from its users. Specifically:

  • We do not collect names, email addresses, or other personal identifiers
  • We do not collect location information
  • We do not use analytics tools to track individual users
  • We do not collect usage data beyond what is described in the Advertising section below

Advertising

Number Buddies displays ads to support the development and maintenance of the app. We use Google AdMob to serve advertisements. When ads are shown:

  • Device or other IDs (including the advertising identifier) may be collected and shared with Google and its advertising partners to deliver and measure ads.
  • Ad requests are configured with child-directed treatment and age-appropriate content settings in compliance with Google Play's Families Policy.
  • Data collected for advertising is encrypted in transit.

You can learn more about how Google uses data in advertising at Google's Privacy & Terms.

Children's Privacy

Number Buddies is designed for young children and fully complies with Google Play's Families Policy Requirements. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Our ad implementation uses child-directed settings to provide age-appropriate advertising.

How The App Works

Number Buddies works offline for game content. All game data, including scores and progress, is stored locally on your device and is never transmitted to us. Ad delivery requires an internet connection when ads are shown.

Changes to Our Privacy Policy

If we make material changes to this Privacy Policy, we will notify you through an update to the app and this document.
